Evaluation Rubrics & Scoring Benchmark Guidelines

Standard 10-Mark Scoring Rubric Reference

Benchmark criteria for Session Chairs to ensure objective and standardized evaluation across technical sessions.

No. Evaluation Parameter Outstanding (9 – 10) Good (7 – 8) Average (5 – 6) Below Average (3 – 4) Inadequate (0 – 2)
1 Relevance, Significance & Novelty Breakthrough problem formulation; original approach with profound scholarly impact. Clear novel contribution with proven industrial or academic value. Incremental modification of existing methods; modest originality. Routine application of known techniques with little novelty. Trivial or plagiarized ideas; no scholarly contribution.
2 Technical Quality & Methodology Mathematically rigorous, robust algorithmic pipeline, comprehensive ablation & proof. Sound technical basis, appropriate test controls, valid assumptions. Basic methodology adequate, but lacks stress testing or deep analysis. Questionable assumptions, noticeable flaws in formulation. Fundamentally flawed methodology, broken equations, unproven claims.
3 Results & Research Contribution Exhaustive benchmarks against state-of-the-art; statistically validated gains. Strong empirical data, good baseline comparisons, clear insights. Results present but baselines are weak or metrics poorly justified. Sparse data, inconclusive metrics, unverified performance. Unsubstantiated claims, fabricated or non-reproducible outcomes.
4 Presentation Quality & Clarity Exemplary slides, masterclass storytelling, flawless timing & articulation. Clear visuals, well-structured narrative, adheres to time budget. Legible slides, reasonable delivery, occasional pacing drift. Cluttered text, rushed or sluggish pacing, exceeds time limit. Incomprehensible slides, inaudible speech, severe time breach.
5 Q&A / Subject Knowledge Authoritative mastery; addresses complex questions with depth and poise. Solid domain knowledge; answers questions convincingly and politely. Answers standard questions adequately; struggles with deeper concepts. Evasive or superficial replies; limited grasp of foundational literature. Unable to answer fundamental questions or defend work.
Session Chair Feedback for Conference Organization Rubrics
1 Session Planning & Coordination Impeccable coordination, punctual start, proactive secretariat support. Smooth session flow, minor delays handled efficiently. Adequate coordination, slight delays in author notifications. Disorganized agenda, significant delays, lack of direction. Session schedule breakdown, missing chair materials.
2 Presentation & Time Management Exact time allocation, precise visual reminders, optimal flow. Good time enforcement with gentle alerts to authors. Acceptable time control; some presentations ran slightly over. Poor time enforcement; session ran severely overtime. No timekeeping; session disrupted by unchecked overruns.
3 Technical/AV & Infrastructure Support State-of-the-art AV, crystal-clear microphones, seamless hybrid stream. Reliable projection and audio; prompt technician assistance. Working AV with minor audio hum or projector adjustment needed. Frequent AV glitches, dropped video calls, delayed technician help. Complete AV failure, inoperable displays, no technical support.
4 Participant & Presenter Management 100% presenter turnout, verified registrations, enthusiastic audience. Good attendance, engaged audience, smooth speaker transitions. Moderate attendance, minor delays in finding registered authors. Multiple no-show authors without notice, low participant engagement. Severe attendee disruption, unmanaged room occupancy.
5 Overall Conference Organization & Support World-class hospitality, prompt desk support, excellent amenities. Professional organization, polite volunteers, well-arranged venue. Satisfactory arrangements meeting basic conference requirements. Understaffed desk, unhelpful assistance, inadequate amenities. Gross mismanagement, complete absence of organizational support.
